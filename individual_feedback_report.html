Assessment information

The BFI consists of 25 personality items and 3 demographics (gender, education, and age). Responses to the 25 personality items determine an individuals standing along 5 personality dimensions:

  • Extraversion – an orientation of interest and energies ranging from the outer world of people and things to the inner world of subjective experience
  • Conscientiousness – a tendency to be organized, responsible, and hardworking/industrious
  • Agreeableness – tendency to act in a cooperative, unselfish manner
  • Openness to experience (Openness) – tendency to be open to new aesthetic, cultural, or intellectual experiences
  • Neuroticism – chronic levels of emotional instability and proneness to psychological distress
